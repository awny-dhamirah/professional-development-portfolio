On the 4 November 2026, I attended a PC assemble lab. During this session I was thought of the correct way of assembling and disassembling a PC and the proper way of handling the components of the PC.

Assembling steps: 
1.	Screw in the CPU fan.
2.	Screw in the hard disk.
3.	Insert the RAM until it clicks into place.
4.	Screw in the power supply unit. 
5.	Attach all the cables.
6.	Close the cover of the PC. 

Handling care of the components:
1.	Do not touch the gold plating of the RAM. 
2.	Ensure cables that have locks are properly locked into place.
